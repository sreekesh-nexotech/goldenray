## Changes Made in This Revision

### 1. Brand error — PAN-003 is not a Tata Power Solar panel
`GSD7S72T-575WT` is a **G-Star Solar** model. Its datasheet is issued by G-Star (`www.gstarsolar.com`,
"Choose Gstar, Grasp the Future!", doc version GS-202406) and ENF's panel directory lists the series
under G-STAR. ComparePV mis-attributes it to Tata Power Solar; that attribution appears to be the
source of the error.

Every spec in the old PAN-003 row (22.26%, −0.30%/°C, 80% bifaciality, 25-year product warranty,
30 years / 87.4%) is correct — it just belongs to G-Star, not Tata. The two asterisks on the warranty
columns are now removed: **25-year product warranty and 30-year / 87.40% linear warranty are both
confirmed** on the G-Star datasheet.

Tata Power Solar's actual glass-glass bifacial module is the **TP600LG10B**, now added as PAN-011. Note
it is **Mono PERC, not TOPCon**, and its −0.37%/°C temperature coefficient is the weakest in this table —
materially different from what the old Tata row implied.

Because "Company Strength 4.9" was assigned on the belief this was Tata, PAN-003's company score is
lowered to 4.0 (G-Star has limited Indian market presence), which moves its rating from Expert's Choice
to Very Good. Adjust if you rate G-Star differently.

### 2. Warranty error — PAN-007 Emmvee is 30 years, not 25
The Emmvee Titanium Clear datasheet states **"YEAR 2-30 POWER DEGRADATION END OF 30 YEARS 87.40%"**.
The old row's "25 years / 89.4%" is outdated. Warranty score raised 4.3 → 4.8.

### 4. Corrected figures
- **PAN-006 efficiency 21.66% → 21.68%.** 560 W over the datasheet's 2278 × 1134 mm (2.5832 m²) is 21.68%.
- **PAN-004 model ID.** The datasheet covers `VSMDH.72.AAA.05` where `AAA = 540–565`; the 550 W unit is
  properly `VSMDH.72.550.05`. Its 21.29% efficiency and the 83.5% year-30 figure both check out
  (100 − 2.0 − 0.5 × 29 = 83.5).
- **PAN-005 temperature coefficient.** −0.2915%/°C is exactly right — it appears as the precise value on
  the RenewSys datasheet, with −0.29%/°C as the rounded headline figure. No change needed.

### 5. Arithmetic
PAN-007's Overall Score was 4.62 but its four pillars (4.7, 4.6, 4.8, 4.3) average to 4.60. The warranty
correction in item 2 supersedes this; the row now reads 4.7 / 4.6 / 4.8 / 4.8 → **4.72**.

Note that Overall Score is not reproducible from the four pillar columns by any single formula across the
original eight rows — most sit ~0.01 below their simple average, PAN-004 matches exactly, and PAN-007 sat
0.02 above. New rows use the simple average. Worth settling on one formula.

### 6. Lifecycle warnings
- **PAN-006 (Premier PE-560 THGB)** is flagged **no longer manufactured** in ENF's directory. Premier's
  current equivalents are the PE 560–590 W THGB 144 series and a newer 132-cell 600–630 W TOPCon module.
- **PAN-008 (Goldi 555 Wp)** is on datasheet v1.3 (2024), covering 555–570 Wp. Goldi has since published a
  **565–595 Wp M10R** revision (v1.1, 2026) — the 555 Wp variant is being phased out.
- **PAN-005 (RenewSys)** datasheet is the Aug-2024 rev 5 covering 565–600 Wp; a newer 565–610 Wp
  183.75 mm variant is also listed.

## New Panels Added

**PAN-009 — Waaree ELITE Series BiN-01-555.** Waaree's N-type TOPCon line, which the old table missed
entirely — PAN-001 is Waaree's older P-type PERC product. G12 108-cell framed dual glass, 1965 × 1303 ×
35 mm, 32.5 kg, 5400/2400 Pa, NOCT 43 ± 2 °C, 35 A fuse. Linear warranty 99.00% yr 1 → 94.6% yr 12 →
87.4% yr 30. Straight upgrade over PAN-001 on heat (−0.30 vs −0.34%/°C) and retention (87.4% vs 82.05%).

**PAN-010 — Saatvik SGE 580-144 TGG.** Highest wattage and efficiency in the table (580 W, 22.45%),
N-TOPCon dual glass, 144 cells, 16BB MBB, bifaciality 80 ± 5%, tolerance 0~+4.99 Wp. Saatvik won a
200 MW N-TOPCon 580 Wp supply contract, so volume availability is real.

**PAN-011 — Tata Power Solar TP600LG10B.** Added to give the table a genuine Tata entry after the PAN-003
correction. Mono PERC bifacial glass-glass, 156 cells, 2456 × 1134 × 35 mm, IP68, 5400/2400 Pa,
NOCT 45 ± 1 °C, 6005-T6 frame, published warranty claim rate under 0.07%. Included with the caveat that
**−0.37%/°C is poor for Kerala's heat** — it is here for brand completeness, not performance.

## Applied to the Codebase

`goldenray-backend/backend/goldenray/management/commands/populate_solar_panels.py` now seeds these
eleven panels. Run it to apply:

```bash
cd goldenray-backend/backend && python manage.py populate_solar_panels
```

The previous seed carried six panels — Waaree Ahnay, Adani Elan Shine, Vikram **Prexos 545 W**, Saatvik
SGE 580, Premier **PE-550HB**, RenewSys **DESERV SGalactic 144 monofacial** — three of which are models
this document does not cover. The command now **prunes** any row not in the seed set, so those stale
records are removed rather than left serving superseded specs alongside the corrected ones. Pass
`--no-prune` to keep them.

Two data-integrity changes went in with it:

- **Adani's temperature coefficient was −0.26%/°C in the seeder.** That is wrong on any reading; it is
  now −0.32%/°C.
- **`output_at_year_25` held year-30 figures.** `RecommendationSection.tsx:82` renders that value into
  the sentence *"…% output even after 25 years"*, so the site was overstating retention. The field now
  holds genuine year-25 values (`100 − first_year_drop − annual_degradation × 24`) and the copy is
  truthful. End-of-warranty figures remain in the table above. If you would rather surface year 30 on
  the site, that is a field rename plus migration — say the word.

`real_output_at_60c` and `efficiency_rating` are now derived by formula rather than hand-picked; the
formulas are documented in the seeder's module docstring. All eleven rows were validated against the
model's field rules (choices, `max_digits`, decimal places, `max_length`) and pass.

Note that `flarize-sreyes/` is a **separate checkout of the same GitHub remote**, last updated 4 Sep
2026. It was left untouched and will pick this up on its next pull.
