# Malayalam translation checklist — quotation PDF

Fill the **Malayalam** column for every row. Anything left blank keeps the
English text, so this can be handed back page by page.

Rules:

- Keep numbers, currency amounts, dates, percentages and units exactly as they
  appear (e.g. 78,000 / 5 kW / 8-12%) — only the words around them change.
- Keep brand names in English: Flarize, Golden Ray, KSEB, MNRE, PM Surya Ghar,
  and all panel/inverter makes and models.
- Tokens in braces such as {name}, {amount}, {min}, {max}, {district}, {year}
  are placeholders: copy them into the Malayalam text unchanged, positioned
  wherever Malayalam word order needs them.
- Header, footer, quote number, GST number and customer details are never
  translated and are not listed here.
- ((remove)) in the Malayalam column means: render nothing there in Malayalam.
  Use it only where the Malayalam layout deliberately drops a label.
